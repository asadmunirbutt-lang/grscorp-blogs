Early Intervention

New York State's Early Intervention (EI) Program provides services to children from birth to age 3 at no cost to families. It is one of the most important and underutilized systems available to families of young autistic children.

Adult Services

The "services cliff" — the dramatic drop in support many autistic individuals experience when they age out of school-based services at 21 — is one of the most critical challenges facing the autism community. Planning ahead matters.

Crisis & Hotlines

When you or someone you love needs immediate support, these resources provide 24/7 access to trained crisis counselors.

Crisis Resources — Available 24/7

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline Call or text 988 — specialized support available for autistic callers and their families. Available 24/7.
Crisis Text Line Text HOME to 741741 — text-based crisis support. Particularly useful for individuals who find phone calls difficult.
NYS OMH Crisis Lines New York State Office of Mental Health maintains regional crisis lines. Find your local line at omh.ny.gov/omhweb/crisis_resources/
OPWDD Crisis Support (NYS) If an individual with a developmental disability is in crisis and currently receiving OPWDD services, contact your Care Manager or voluntary provider's crisis line immediately.
National Alliance on Mental Illness (NAMI) Helpline Call 1-800-950-NAMI (6264) Monday–Friday 10am–10pm ET for information, referrals, and support. NAMI-NYS: naminys.org
