🚨 Warning signs — call doctor / go to ER ▶
📞 Call care team if any of these:
  • 🌡️ Fever 100.4°F (38°C) or higher
  • 🫄 Belly pain getting worse or coming back
  • 🤢 Nausea, vomiting, or unable to tolerate feeds
  • 🔴 Redness, swelling, warmth, pus, bad smell, or bleeding at G-tube site — or formula leaking around tube
  • 🔧 G-tube clogged after flushing, or tube moves in/out more than usual
  • 💩 Greasy, oily, pale, or very bad-smelling stools — or more diarrhea than usual, or constipation >48 hrs
  • 🩸 Signs of high blood sugar: very thirsty, peeing much more than usual, waking at night to pee, tiredness, or weight loss
  • 🟡 Yellow skin or eyes, dark urine
🚨 Go to ER or call 911 if:
  • 🫄 Severe belly pain or a hard, swollen belly
  • 🩸 Vomiting blood or green fluid — or black/bloody stools
  • 💨 Trouble breathing, fast heartbeat, fainting, or confusion
  • 💧 Signs of dehydration: no pee for 8+ hours, very dry mouth, no tears, very sleepy
  • 🔧 G-tube comes out — go to ER right away
  • 🍬 Fruity breath, deep fast breathing, or vomiting along with high blood sugar
📋 G-tube care reminders:
  • Avoid placing gauze underneath the bumper
  • Do NOT deflate balloon or exchange G-tube until 6 weeks post-op
