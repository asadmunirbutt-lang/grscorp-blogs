🚀 Getting Started
🔑 First Login
  1. 1Open the app link and enter your work email.
  2. 2Check your email for a password reset link — click it to create your password.
  3. 3On the registration page, take a selfie / profile photo — this is required.
  4. 4Set your password and tap Create Account.
  5. 5You're in! Your photo and name appear in the top bar.
📅 Selecting Your Shift
🌅 Morning ☀️ Midday 🌙 Night
🏖️ Saturday 🌿 Sunday
  • Tap your shift at the start of every session.
  • Once selected it locks — you can't accidentally switch mid-shift.
  • Sign out and back in to start a new shift.
✅ Room Checklist
Main workflow: Select a floor → select a room → mark each task → submit. Work through one room at a time.
✅ Yes
  • Task is done.
  • A timestamp is saved the moment you tap — admin can see exactly when each task was completed.
🚫 Not Needed
  • Task was not applicable this shift (e.g. room not in use).
  • Still counts toward room completion.
⏳ Later
  • Cannot complete right now — requires a note explaining why.
  • Appears as a Carry Over badge for the next shift.
  • Two missed shifts = red CRITICAL badge.
📤 Submitting a Room
  • Once all tasks are marked, tap Submit Room Tasks.
  • A green confirmation card shows the submission time.
  • If you change a task after submitting, the room resets and you must submit again.
💡 Tips
  • Some tasks only appear on specific days (e.g. fridge expiry check: Mon & Fri only).
  • Use the quick nav bar at the top to jump to Notes, Supplies, Food Log, or Laundry without scrolling.
  • The room name sticks to the top while you scroll through long task lists.
🧺 Laundry Log
Logging a Laundry Event
  1. 1Choose a Category: 👕 Clothes 🧹 Cleaning 🛏️ Linen
  2. 2Tap the event: 🫧 Wash = started washer  |  🌀 Dryer = started dryer  |  👕 Fold = folding complete
  3. 3Entry is saved instantly with your name and time. Tap 📋 History to view past entries.
📝 Notes
Adding a Note
  • Type a message in the Notes box — any observation, reminder, or handover info for the next shift.
  • Optionally attach a photo (camera or gallery).
  • Tap Post Note. Your note appears at the top.
🚨 Critical Notes
  • Before posting, tap ⚠️ Mark as Critical if the note needs immediate attention by the next shift.
  • A pulsing red banner appears at the top of the app for the next shift to see.
  • The next shift must tap 👁️ Acknowledge — the banner then disappears.
  • Use sparingly — only for genuine urgent issues.
🛒 Supplies Needed
Requesting a Supply
  1. 1Enter the item name and quantity needed.
  2. 2Tap Add Supply Request. Status starts as ⏳ Pending.
  3. 3Admin will update status to ✅ Ordered once actioned.
🍽️ Azlan's Food Log
Logging a Meal
  1. 1Select the meal from the dropdown: 🍜 Noodles 🍗 Nuggets 🍟 Fries 🍞 Chocolate Toast 🍪 Snacks
  2. 2Enter the quantity and any optional notes (e.g. "added ketchup", "only ate half").
  3. 3Tap 🍽️ Log Meal. Entry is saved with your name and time.
Log every meal and snack — this helps the family track Azlan's daily intake and preferences.
⚙️ Admin Only
📅 Daily & 📊 Weekly Reports
  • View all room submissions across all staff for any day.
  • See task-by-task completion with timestamps.
  • Weekly view shows trends across all shifts.
⚙️ Admin Panel
  • Staff tab — create new accounts for caretakers (admin stays logged in).
  • Rooms tab — add or delete tasks for any room.
  • Delete entries — ✕ button appears on laundry, food log, notes, and supplies for admin.
  • Shift — tap any shift at any time; never locks.
Admin reminder: When creating a new staff account, use their work email. They will receive a password reset email to set their own password and take their profile photo on first login.
📋 House Rules for App Use
✔ Do
  • Select your shift first thing when you start work.
  • Mark tasks as you do them — timestamps help admin verify timing.
  • Log every laundry event (start of wash, start of dryer, folding done).
  • Log every meal Azlan eats with quantity.
  • Add a note if anything unusual happened during your shift.
  • Mark notes as Critical only for urgent handover issues.
✘ Don't
  • Don't submit a room without marking every task.
  • Don't use ⏳ Later without writing a clear reason.
  • Don't share your login credentials with anyone.
  • Don't log meals on behalf of another shift — log only what you personally served.
  • Don't ignore a red 🚨 Critical banner — acknowledge it immediately.
