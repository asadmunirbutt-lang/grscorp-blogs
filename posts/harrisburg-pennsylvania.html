What Makes a Venue Sensory-Friendly?

  • Reduced Noise Levels: Quieter environments with lower volumes or muted announcements
  • Sensory-Friendly Hours: Special times with dimmer lights, reduced crowds, and no flashing effects
  • Sensory Kits Available: Noise-canceling headphones, fidgets, and other calming tools
  • Trained Staff: Employees understand autism and sensory needs; accommodations available
