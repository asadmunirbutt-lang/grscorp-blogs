Our Services

Every tool and resource GRSCorp provides is free. No subscriptions, no paywalls — just support when families need it most.

What GRSCorp Offers

Five core services designed to guide families from initial questions through evaluation, resources, and ongoing support.

📋
Free Autism Self-Assessment Screening Tool

A 10-question screening questionnaire based on common autism spectrum traits. Takes 5–10 minutes to complete. Provides a scored result and personalized recommendations — including a curated list of evaluation specialists in your state.

This is a screening tool only, not a diagnosis. It helps families decide whether to pursue a formal professional evaluation.

Autism Specialist Directory

A curated directory of developmental pediatricians, clinical psychologists, and neuropsychologists who specialize in autism evaluation. Currently covering 10+ states including New York, California, Texas, Florida, Washington, Illinois, Massachusetts, Pennsylvania, Colorado, and Arizona.

Each listing includes specialty, city, phone number, and telehealth availability.

Educational Rights & IEP Guides

State-specific guides helping parents understand their rights within the public school system. Topics include Individualized Education Programs (IEPs), out-of-district placement, private school alternatives, extended school year services, and how to advocate effectively at IEP meetings.

Currently focused on New York State, with additional state guides in development.
