About Global Relief And Support Corporation

We believe every family deserves access to the tools and knowledge they need to navigate the autism journey.

Our Mission

Global Relief And Support Corporation exists to eliminate the barriers families face when seeking autism support. We provide free, accessible resources — from initial screening to specialist connections — so no family feels lost or alone.

Our work is grounded in the belief that every person on the autism spectrum deserves timely evaluation, honest information, and a clear path to the support they need.

Our Vision

A future where geography, income, and lack of information are no longer obstacles to autism support. Where every family — regardless of background — can access quality evaluation and resources from day one.

We serve families across the United States, with a focus on connecting people to local specialists and state-specific educational resources.

What We Do

GRSCorp provides a suite of free tools and resources designed for families at every stage of the autism journey.

Autism Screening Tools

Our free self-assessment questionnaire helps individuals and families explore whether professional evaluation may be beneficial.

Specialist Directories

Curated lists of developmental pediatricians and psychologists across 10+ states, including telehealth options.

Educational Guides

State-specific guides on IEPs, out-of-district school placements, parent rights, and legal advocacy strategies.

Expert Blog

In-depth articles written to help families understand the systems and processes involved in autism support and education.

Family Support

Resources designed to reduce the emotional and logistical burden on families navigating autism evaluation and care.

Always Free

Every resource, tool, and guide is free — because access to support should never depend on ability to pay.

Our Values

These principles guide everything we build and every resource we create.

Accessibility

All resources are free, mobile-friendly, and written in plain language. Support should never be gated behind a paywall or jargon.

Accuracy

We cite credible sources, reference established screening tools, and are transparent about the limits of self-assessment.

Compassion

We build every resource with the understanding that families seeking autism support are navigating a difficult and emotional process.

Simplicity

We strip away complexity. Every tool, page, and guide is designed to be clear, direct, and immediately actionable.
