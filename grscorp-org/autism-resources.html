Common Questions

Answers to the questions families ask most when starting the autism evaluation journey.

Is the autism screening tool a diagnosis?

No. The GRSCorp screening tool is a self-assessment questionnaire, not a clinical diagnosis. It helps identify whether professional evaluation may be beneficial. Only a licensed healthcare professional can diagnose autism spectrum disorder.

What age can take the autism screening?

Our screening tool is designed for ages 6 and older. For children under 6, we recommend speaking directly with your pediatrician, who can refer you to a developmental specialist.

How do I get an official autism evaluation?

Start by speaking with your primary care physician or pediatrician. They can provide a referral to a developmental pediatrician, clinical psychologist, or neuropsychologist who specializes in autism assessment. You can also use our specialist directory to find providers in your state.

What is an IEP and does my child qualify?

An Individualized Education Program (IEP) is a legally binding document that outlines the special education services a child is entitled to receive in public school. Children with autism often qualify. The process starts with requesting an evaluation from your school district in writing.

Are GRSCorp resources really free?

Yes. Every tool, guide, and article on GRSCorp is completely free. We do not charge for access, require subscriptions, or sell user data.
