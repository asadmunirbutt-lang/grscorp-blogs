Our Mission

At Global Relief And Support Corporation (GRS Corp), we're passionate about making a positive impact in the lives of individuals with autism, fostering community awareness, and creating bright opportunities for all.

Our goal is to enhance the quality of life for individuals with autism, enabling them to fully participate in their local communities — through free resources, advocacy, and direct family support.

Read more about our mission →

Our Core Values

  • Compassion — meeting families where they are
  • Advocacy — fighting for every child's right to thrive
  • Community — building spaces of belonging
  • Transparency — clinically accurate, unbiased guidance
