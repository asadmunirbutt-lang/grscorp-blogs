Autism Early Signs Checklist

Ages 12 Months to 5 Years

Observable developmental behaviors that may indicate the need for an autism evaluation. Organized by age group. Use this checklist to track observations and start a conversation with your child's doctor. This checklist is not a diagnostic tool.

Important Disclaimer — Please Read This checklist is for educational and observational purposes only. It is NOT a diagnostic tool and cannot determine whether a child has autism. Only qualified clinical professionals (developmental pediatricians, child psychologists, neuropsychologists) can diagnose autism through a comprehensive evaluation. Checking multiple items on this list is not a diagnosis, and checking few or no items does not rule out autism. If you have any concerns about your child's development at any age, talk to your pediatrician and request a referral for evaluation. Early evaluation is always appropriate when there are concerns — there is no downside to seeking more information.

Remember: Autistic children vary enormously. Some develop language typically but have significant social differences. Others have language delays without other autism traits. Some of these items occur in typical development occasionally — it is the pattern, persistence, and functional impact that matters clinically. This checklist captures common observations; a qualified evaluator will assess far more comprehensively.

What to Do If You Have Concerns

  1. Talk to your pediatrician at your next well-child visit — bring this checklist with items noted. Ask for a referral to a developmental pediatrician or child psychologist for a comprehensive autism evaluation.
  2. Don't wait for the next scheduled visit if you're concerned now. Call the office and ask for an urgent developmental concern appointment. Concerns about regression are especially urgent.
  3. Request an Early Intervention evaluation (if your child is under 3) — call New York State's EI hotline: 1-800-522-5006. Referrals are free and don't require a doctor's referral.
  4. Request a CPSE evaluation from your school district (if your child is 3–5) — contact the special education office of your local school district to request a preschool evaluation at no cost.
  5. Read our full guide on How to Get an Autism Evaluation and Early Signs of Autism in Young Children.
  6. Visit our Autism Resources Hub for evaluation support, early intervention information, school advocacy guides, and community resources.
