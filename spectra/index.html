Frequently Asked Questions

Is SPECTRA a replacement for professional help?
No. SPECTRA is a companion and support tool, not a therapist or doctor. For medical or psychological concerns, always work with qualified professionals. SPECTRA helps bridge gaps and provides 24/7 support between appointments.
Is my information private?
Yes. Your conversations are private and secure. We don't sell data. We're a nonprofit, not a tech company monetizing user information. See our privacy policy for details.
How is SPECTRA different from ChatGPT?
SPECTRA is specifically trained for autism caregiving. It understands the emotional and practical realities of raising autistic children. It has real-time web search for current resources. And it's backed by a nonprofit mission, not profit incentives.
Is it really free?
Completely free. No subscription, no ads, no hidden costs. SPECTRA is funded by GRSCorp, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to supporting autistic individuals and families.
What if I disagree with something SPECTRA says?
That's okay. SPECTRA is a tool to help you think through situations, not the final word. Always trust your instincts as a parent and consult professionals when needed. We welcome feedback.
